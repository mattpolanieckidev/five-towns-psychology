Meet the Founder

About Five Towns Psychology

A warm, collaborative approach to evidence-based mental health care for children, adolescents, and adults.

Dr. Avital Soep

Compassionate care grounded in evidence.

Dr. Avital Soep is a licensed clinical psychologist in the state of New York. Avital received her doctorate in School-Clinical Child Psychology from Yeshiva University, Ferkauf Graduate School of Psychology.

Prior to attending Ferkauf, she graduated Summa Cum Laude with a B.A. in psychology from Yeshiva University, Stern College for Women. Throughout her graduate training, Avital has worked with children, adolescents, and adults in schools, outpatient clinics, inpatient hospitals, and private practice.

Clinical approachDr. Soep is trained and experienced in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) techniques.

Our Approach

Making high-quality mental health care accessible.

Dr. Soep is passionate about the implementation and dissemination of evidence-based interventions, making the highest quality of mental health care accessible for all.

A primary goal is to help remove the stigma surrounding mental health care by introducing emotion-focused learning and skills-based work to children from a young age in an engaging and effective manner.

Her clinical experience includes work with depression, social anxiety, generalized anxiety, ADHD, borderline personality disorder, and posttraumatic stress disorder.
